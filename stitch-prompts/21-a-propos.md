# Page — À Propos (About)

Purpose: tells the story of the house — founder, craftsmanship, philosophy, workshop, values, vision. Builds the brand.

```text
Design the ANGALY "À propos" page — the brand story page for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar with "À propos" active.

SECTION 1 — HERO:
Full-width photo of the atelier or founder at work, deep navy gradient overlay at bottom, serif headline "Notre histoire", italic subheading "Une maison de couture née à Madagascar, pensée pour durer."

SECTION 2 — NOTRE HISTOIRE:
Ivory background, two-column layout: left a black-and-white or warm-toned archival-style photo, right a serif subheading "Comment tout a commencé" and 2–3 narrative paragraphs, optionally with a small vertical chronology (year markers in champagne with short milestone labels).

SECTION 3 — LA FONDATRICE ("Qui est Angaly ?"):
A warm, human section: large portrait photo on one side, on the other side a serif subheading "Qui est Angaly ?", a short bio paragraph covering parcours, passion, expérience, spécialités, and an italic pull-quote from the founder about her vision of fashion.

SECTION 4 — NOTRE SAVOIR-FAIRE:
A three or four-column feature row with small icons/photos illustrating: Couture main, Broderie, Patronage sur mesure, Finitions artisanales — each with a short serif title and one-line description.

SECTION 5 — NOTRE PHILOSOPHIE:
A centered, editorial full-width quote block on a deep navy (#0C2650) background: large italic serif statement about the brand's philosophy, in white/ivory text, champagne quotation marks as a subtle decorative accent.

SECTION 6 — NOTRE ATELIER:
A photo gallery of the atelier space and craftsmanship in progress (measuring, cutting, sewing, fitting), 4–6 images in an editorial grid.

SECTION 7 — NOS VALEURS:
A grid of value cards (Excellence, Authenticité, Exclusivité, Proximité client, or similar), each with a small line-icon in champagne, short title, one-line description.

SECTION 8 — NOTRE VISION:
A closing serif statement about the future/vision of the house, followed by a Primary button "Découvrir nos créations" and Secondary button "Prendre rendez-vous".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
All two-column sections stack vertically (photo above text), chronology becomes a simple vertical timeline, galleries become swipeable.

AVOID:
generic corporate "about us" template feel, stock-photo look, excessive gold, heavy shadows, overly long unbroken text blocks without visual breathing room.

The page should feel intimate, human, and prestigious at once — the reader should come away trusting the craftsmanship and the person behind it.
```
