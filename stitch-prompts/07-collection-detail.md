# Page — Détail d'une Collection

Purpose: dedicated editorial page for one collection, telling its story and showcasing all associated creations.

```text
Design the ANGALY collection detail page — the dedicated page for a single couture collection, for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar. Breadcrumb: Accueil / Collections / Collection Éternelle.

SECTION 1 — CINEMATIC COVER:
Full-width, large-height cover photograph of the collection's key look, deep navy gradient overlay only at the bottom, containing: small label "COLLECTION 2026", large serif title "Collection Éternelle", italic serif tagline describing the collection's mood.

SECTION 2 — STORY:
Ivory background, two-column layout: left a portrait photo (mood/atelier shot), right a serif subheading "L'histoire de la collection" and 2–3 paragraphs about the inspiration, followed by an optional embedded video thumbnail with a play icon overlay ("Voir la vidéo de la collection").

SECTION 3 — GALLERY OF CREATIONS:
Serif subheading "Les créations de la collection", an editorial masonry grid of all creations belonging to this collection (reuse the creation card style: image, category tag, serif title, "Voir la création" link).

SECTION 4 — CLOSING CTA:
Deep navy (#0C2650) full-width band, white/ivory serif text "Vous souhaitez porter une pièce de cette collection ?" with Primary button "Prendre rendez-vous" and Secondary outlined button "Voir toutes les collections".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Cover image stays full-bleed and tall; story section stacks vertically (photo above text); gallery becomes single-column with full-width images; sticky "Prendre rendez-vous" CTA at bottom.

AVOID:
generic product-listing style for the gallery, excessive gold, heavy shadows, cluttered typography.

The page should feel like a dedicated exhibition catalogue page for the collection.
```
