# Page — Angaly Pattern Studio (Landing / Overview)

Purpose: the premium landing page introducing the flagship differentiating feature — an AI-assisted parametric pattern-making atelier. Must feel like a sophisticated digital atelier, not a cold SaaS product.

```text
Design the "Angaly Pattern Studio" landing page — the premium digital pattern-making atelier for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar. This module has a slightly distinct, more premium identity while remaining clearly part of the ANGALY brand.

PATTERN STUDIO PALETTE (use exactly, distinct from the general site palette):
- Background: #041329 (deep, almost black navy)
- Primary: #061938
- Surface (cards/panels): #0C2650
- Accent: #C5B190 (champagne — for highlights, active states, key numbers)
- Premium Gold: #936C3E (for premium button and premium badges only, used sparingly)
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Secondary text: #D8D3C8 (warm ivory)
Balance: this module is allowed to be darker/moodier than the main site (it is a "night atelier" feel), but avoid making it pure black or neon-lit — it must still read as warm and premium, not like a generic dark-mode dashboard.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display), used for the module name and section titles. Body/UI: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope).

BUTTONS:
Premium button: background #936C3E, text #FFFFFF, hover #B59A70 — used for the main "start" actions. Secondary button on dark background: transparent with a #C5B190 border and #C5B190 text.

TOP NAVIGATION:
A refined dark header (#041329) with the ANGALY logo (in white/champagne) plus a small "Pattern Studio" wordmark/badge next to it, a minimal set of links (Retour au site, Mes projets, Aide), and an account icon.

SECTION 1 — HERO:
Full-width dark hero (#041329 background) with a subtle textured or softly blurred background image evoking pattern paper / technical drafting lines in low-opacity champagne, or a moody photo of pattern pieces on a cutting table. Centered content: small champagne label "PREMIUM", large serif headline "Angaly Pattern Studio", italic serif subheading "Votre patron, créé selon vos mesures.", a short paragraph in #D8D3C8 explaining: "Un moteur de patronage paramétrique assisté par intelligence artificielle, pensé comme un véritable atelier numérique — pas une simple IA qui dessine à votre place." Premium button "Nouveau projet" and Secondary (outlined champagne) button "Découvrir le fonctionnement".

SECTION 2 — HOW IT WORKS (11-step overview):
Serif subheading in white "Comment ça fonctionne", presented as a refined horizontal (vertical on mobile) stepper across two rows or a compact numbered list on dark surface cards (#0C2650), each step with a small champagne-outlined icon, step number, and short label:
1. Nouveau projet
2. Type de vêtement
3. Style
4. Personnalisation
5. Photo d'inspiration
6. Mesures
7. Génération
8. Prévisualisation
9. Vérification Angaly
10. Validation
11. Export PDF

SECTION 3 — TRUST/POSITIONING BLOCK:
A two-column section: left a short manifesto in italic serif, "L'intelligence artificielle comme assistante du patronage professionnel — jamais comme un remplacement de la couturière."; right, three small trust points with champagne icons: "Précision paramétrique" (moteur géométrique déterministe), "Vérification humaine" (contrôle par une couturière Angaly avant validation), "Export professionnel" (PDF A4/A3/A0, SVG, DXF).

SECTION 4 — SAMPLE PATTERN PREVIEW:
A dark surface card (#0C2650) showing a stylized preview of technical pattern pieces (devant, dos, manche) rendered elegantly with fine white/champagne outline linework on the dark background, labeled with small technical annotations (droit-fil, marges de couture, crans) to convey precision and craftsmanship.

SECTION 5 — PRICING TIERS:
Three pricing cards on dark surfaces (#0C2650), middle one highlighted with a thin champagne border and a "Recommandé" badge: "Patron numérique" (génération, prévisualisation, export), "Patron + vérification Angaly" (+ contrôle professionnel, corrections, validation), "Patron + confection" (+ confection complète, essayage, livraison). Each card ends with a Premium button "Choisir cette offre".

SECTION 6 — CLOSING CTA:
Centered, serif headline "Prêt·e à créer votre patron ?", Premium button "Nouveau projet".

FOOTER:
A dark (#041329) minimal footer: link back to the main ANGALY site, legal links, and the standard social icons.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Steps become a vertical list with a connecting line; pricing cards stack full-width; hero text remains legible over the background art.

AVOID:
looking like a generic AI SaaS dashboard, neon/cyberpunk tech aesthetics, cold sterile UI, overuse of gold (keep it as accent only), heavy drop shadows, glassmorphism clichés.

The result should evoke a high-end digital pattern-making atelier at night — precise, quietly luxurious, and technologically credible.
```
