# Page — Nos Créations (Full Gallery)

Purpose: the complete browsable gallery of all Angaly creations, across all categories, with filtering. This is the main discovery/catalogue page for creations (distinct from the boutique/ready-to-wear catalogue).

```text
Design the ANGALY "Nos Créations" gallery page — the full creations gallery for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Balance ~60% ivory, 25% navy, 10% neutral, 5% gold/champagne. Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white, Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar with "Nos Créations" active, search/favorites/account icons, and Primary "Prendre rendez-vous" button.

PAGE HEADER:
Ivory background, serif headline "Nos Créations", short sans-serif intro line: "Explorez l'ensemble de notre savoir-faire, des robes de mariée aux costumes sur mesure." Breadcrumb in small slate text: Accueil / Nos Créations.

FILTER BAR (sticky under header on scroll):
A refined horizontal filter bar (not a bulky sidebar) with dropdown/pill filters for:
- Genre: Femme, Homme
- Type: Mariage, Soirée, Costume, Sur mesure, Collection, Accessoires
- Catégorie: Robes de mariée, Costumes homme, Costards sur mesure, Robes de soirée, Robes événementielles, Tenues traditionnelles revisitées, Créations spéciales, Accessoires, Collections
- Couleur (small color swatch chips)
- Style (classique, moderne, glamour, minimaliste, traditionnel)
- Événement
A right-aligned sort control ("Nouveautés", "Populaires", "Alphabétique") and a grid/masonry view toggle icon pair. Active filters appear as small removable chips below the bar with a "Réinitialiser les filtres" text link.

RESULTS COUNT:
Small slate-gray text: "128 créations" style counter above the grid.

GALLERY GRID:
A refined masonry/editorial grid (mix of portrait and square image ratios, not perfectly uniform) of creation cards. Each card contains: large photograph with a soft hover zoom, a small category tag pill (e.g. "Robe de mariée"), the creation title in serif below the image, and on hover a subtle overlay revealing a small "Aperçu rapide" (quick view) icon button and a heart/favorite icon in the top-right corner of the image. Minimal borders (#D9D4CA), no heavy shadows, generous spacing between cards.

QUICK VIEW MODAL (state to include):
When "Aperçu rapide" is triggered, show a modal/panel on ivory background with: image on one side, on the other side the title (serif), category, collection name, short description, and two buttons — Primary "Voir la création" and Secondary "Ajouter aux favoris" (heart icon).

LOAD MORE / PAGINATION:
Below the grid, a centered Secondary button "Voir plus de créations" or a minimal pagination control in slate/navy tones — avoid heavy pagination bars.

EMPTY STATE (for a filtered result with zero matches):
Centered illustration or icon in champagne tone, serif message "Aucune création ne correspond à ces filtres pour le moment.", and a Secondary button "Réinitialiser les filtres".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer (deep navy #041329, white text, column links, newsletter reminder, copyright + language switcher).

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Filters collapse into a single "Filtrer" button opening a full-screen ivory filter panel with the same options stacked vertically and a sticky "Voir les résultats" button at the bottom. Gallery becomes a clean 2-column or single-column touch-friendly grid with large tap targets and swipeable quick previews.

AVOID:
cluttered filter sidebars, marketplace-style dense grids, excessive gold, neon colors, heavy shadows, generic e-commerce card styling with visible price tags (creations are not simple products), flashy hover animations.

The page should feel like browsing a curated, artisanal portfolio — refined and editorial — never like a generic product marketplace.
```
