# Page — Prêt-à-porter (Ready-to-wear Catalogue)

Purpose: catalogue of garments available for immediate purchase or fitting reservation, distinct from bespoke creations.

```text
Design the ANGALY "Prêt-à-porter" catalogue page — the ready-to-wear boutique catalogue for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory. Even though this page has commercial features (price, cart), it must still feel like a couture boutique, not a mass-market e-commerce site.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar with "Prêt-à-porter" active, plus a small cart icon with item-count badge added to the right-side icon group (search, favorites, account, cart, "Prendre rendez-vous").

PAGE HEADER:
Ivory background, serif headline "Prêt-à-porter", sans-serif subtext: "Des pièces raffinées, disponibles immédiatement, sans compromis sur l'élégance."

FILTER BAR:
Refined horizontal filters: Catégorie (Robes, Costumes, Accessoires...), Taille, Couleur (swatches), Matière, Disponibilité (Disponible, Dernière pièce, Sur commande), Prix (range slider styled minimally in navy/champagne), and a sort control (Nouveautés, Prix croissant, Prix décroissant, Populaires).

PRODUCT GRID:
A clean grid (3–4 columns desktop, 2 mobile) of product cards, each with: product photo (portrait ratio), small status badge in the corner when relevant (e.g. "Dernière pièce" in warning tone #A47735, "Épuisé" in muted gray with reduced image opacity, "Réservé" in slate), product name in serif, reference code in small slate text, price in navy medium-weight sans-serif, small color swatch dots, and a heart/favorite icon top-right of the image. On hover, reveal a slim "Aperçu rapide" button overlay.

STATUS BADGE STYLES:
- Disponible: no badge or a subtle champagne dot
- Dernière pièce: small warning-toned pill (#A47735 text on light background)
- Épuisé: gray pill, image shown at reduced opacity, card slightly desaturated
- Sur commande: soft-navy pill "Sur commande — délai 3 semaines"
- Réservé: slate pill "Réservé"

PAGINATION:
Minimal pagination or a centered "Voir plus" secondary button.

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Filters collapse into a "Filtrer" button opening a full-screen panel; 2-column product grid; sticky floating cart icon bottom-right; large touch targets on favorite icons.

AVOID:
loud discount banners, neon sale badges, cluttered comparison tables, generic marketplace visual noise, excessive gold, heavy shadows.

The catalogue should feel like a curated boutique corner of a couture house, elegant and restrained even while displaying prices and availability.
```
