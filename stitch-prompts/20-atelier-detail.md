# Page — Fiche Atelier (Atelier Detail)

Purpose: dedicated page for a single workshop location, useful also for local SEO.

```text
Design the ANGALY atelier detail page — a dedicated page for a single workshop location, for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar. Breadcrumb: Accueil / Ateliers / Atelier Antananarivo Centre.

SECTION 1 — HERO GALLERY:
A wide photo gallery band (main large photo + 2–3 smaller thumbnails) of the atelier's interior and exterior, with a serif title overlay or directly beneath: "Atelier Antananarivo Centre".

SECTION 2 — INFO PANEL (two columns):
Left: full address, phone number (with click-to-call), opening hours listed day by day (e.g. "Lundi–Vendredi : 9h–18h", "Samedi : 9h–13h", "Dimanche : Fermé"), and a services list with small icons: Essayages, Consultations sur mesure, Retouches, Retrait de commandes, Vérification de patrons Premium.
Right: an embedded map snippet with the atelier pin, and buttons: Primary "Prendre rendez-vous dans cet atelier" and Secondary "Voir l'itinéraire".

SECTION 3 — GALLERY / AMBIANCE:
Serif subheading "L'atelier en images", a small editorial grid of 4–6 photos showing the space, tools, fabrics, and craftsmanship in progress.

SECTION 4 — TEAM AT THIS ATELIER (optional):
A row of small team member cards (photo, first name, role, e.g. "Couturière senior") if relevant.

SECTION 5 — LOCAL SEO CONTENT BLOCK:
A short editorial paragraph naturally mentioning the location context (e.g. services de couturière et robe de mariée sur mesure dans ce quartier), styled as normal readable content, not keyword-stuffed.

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Gallery becomes a swipeable carousel; info panel stacks vertically; sticky "Prendre rendez-vous" CTA at the bottom.

AVOID:
generic "store locator" UI, cluttered info density, excessive gold, heavy shadows.

The page should feel like a warm personal introduction to a specific atelier, useful for both visitors and local search.
```
