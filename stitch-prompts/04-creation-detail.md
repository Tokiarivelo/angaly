# Page — Détail d'une Création (Creation Detail)

Purpose: dedicated page for a single creation (e.g. a wedding dress or suit), showing full gallery, craftsmanship details and conversion actions (appointment, personalization, favorites).

```text
Design the ANGALY creation detail page — the dedicated page for a single couture creation, for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white hover #0C2650. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory. Premium button (only for the "personalize/AI" entry point if shown) #936C3E background, white text, hover #B59A70.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar, breadcrumb below it in small slate text: Accueil / Nos Créations / Robes de mariée / Robe Éternelle.

SECTION 1 — GALLERY:
Left/main column (roughly 60% width on desktop): a large primary image viewer showing the creation, with a vertical or horizontal filmstrip of thumbnails below/beside it for: vue de face, vue arrière, détails (broderie, finition), photo portée, coulisses. Support a lightbox/zoom state on click. On mobile this becomes a full-bleed swipeable image carousel with dot indicators.

SECTION 2 — INFORMATION PANEL (sticky on desktop, right column ~40%):
- Small category/collection tag pill (e.g. "Collection Éternelle — Robe de mariée") in champagne-on-ivory styling.
- Serif title, large: "Robe Éternelle"
- Short descriptive paragraph in body sans-serif.
- A clean spec list with labels in slate gray and values in navy: Type, Matière (e.g. "Satin duchesse, dentelle Calais"), Techniques (e.g. "Broderie main, perles"), Disponibilité (badge: Disponible / Sur demande / Pièce unique), Possibilité de reproduction ou personnalisation (yes/no indicator).
- Divider line (#D9D4CA).
- Primary action button, full width: "Prendre rendez-vous"
- Secondary action button, full width: "Créer une version personnalisée" (leads to the personalization flow)
- Two smaller secondary icon-text actions side by side: "Ajouter aux favoris" (heart icon) and "Partager" (share icon)
- A small text link: "Demander une création similaire"

SECTION 3 — CRAFTSMANSHIP STORY:
Full-width ivory band beneath the two-column layout: serif subheading "Le savoir-faire derrière cette création", a short editorial paragraph about materials and technique, accompanied by 2–3 small "coulisses" (behind-the-scenes) photographs of the making process (measuring, sewing, embroidery, fitting) in a horizontal row.

SECTION 4 — RELATED COLLECTION:
Serif subheading "Fait partie de la collection", a horizontal scroll row of 3–4 other creations from the same collection, each as a compact card with image, title, and category, linking onward.

SECTION 5 — YOU MAY ALSO LIKE:
Serif subheading "Vous aimerez aussi", a grid of 3–4 similar creations (same category) as compact cards.

SECTION 6 — APPOINTMENT CTA BAND:
A deep navy (#0C2650) full-width closing band: white/ivory serif text "Envie d'essayer cette création ?" with a Primary (white on navy) button "Prendre rendez-vous".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Gallery becomes a full-width swipeable carousel at the top, followed by the information panel stacked below (no longer sticky), with a sticky bottom action bar containing "Prendre rendez-vous" (primary, most prominent) and a smaller favorites heart icon button.

AVOID:
visible price tags framed like a generic retail product page, cluttered spec tables, excessive gold, heavy shadows, marketplace-style "add to cart" language (this is a couture creation, not a simple SKU).

The page should read like a museum/editorial piece description combined with a refined product page — informative, tactile, and premium.
```
