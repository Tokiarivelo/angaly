# Page — La Une (Editorial Showcase)

Purpose: a standalone editorial page dedicated to Angaly's most exceptional and current creations — the digital equivalent of a fashion magazine's front cover section. Must be visually the most striking page on the site.

```text
Design the ANGALY "La Une" page — a full editorial showcase page for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant brand color), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents only), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate text #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Balance: ~60% ivory, 25% navy, 10% neutral, 5% champagne/gold. Headings in an elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display), body/UI in a clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button: navy background #061938 / white text, hover #0C2650. Secondary button: transparent/ivory with navy border and text.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Reuse the ANGALY sticky navigation bar: logo left, links (Accueil, La Une, Nos Créations, Prêt-à-porter, Sur Mesure, Patron Premium, À propos, Ateliers, Journal, Contact) with "La Une" shown in an active/underlined state, right-side icons (search, favorites, account) and a Primary "Prendre rendez-vous" button.

PAGE HEADER:
Full-width editorial header on ivory background: small eyebrow label "ÉDITORIAL" in champagne uppercase letter-spaced text, large serif headline "LA UNE", italic serif subheading "Les créations qui incarnent l'univers Angaly — sélectionnées et renouvelées par la maison." A thin horizontal rule in champagne beneath.

FEATURED HERO ITEM:
One dominant, large full-bleed photograph (taller than the rest, editorial magazine-cover style) representing the current "Création du mois" or "Collection du moment". Overlaid caption panel (bottom-left, deep navy translucent panel with white/ivory text) containing: category tag ("Collection du moment" / "Coup de cœur" / "Mariage" / "Costume" / "Événement" / "Coulisses" as a small pill badge), serif title (e.g. "Collection Éclat"), one-line description, and a Secondary button "Voir la création" (ivory outline on the dark overlay).

EDITORIAL GRID (main content):
Below the featured item, an asymmetrical masonry-style editorial grid of 6 to 10 additional "La Une" items, mixing portrait and landscape image ratios of different sizes to avoid a repetitive uniform grid (true editorial layout, like a fashion magazine spread, not an e-commerce catalogue). Each item card shows: large photograph, small category label (Mariage / Costume / Collection / Coup de cœur / Coulisses), serif title, one-line description or short story snippet, publish/feature date in small slate gray text, and a subtle text link "Voir la création →". Cards should have minimal borders, no heavy shadows, generous gutter spacing between images.

SECONDARY DETAIL — STORY SNIPPET:
For 1–2 of the items, include an expanded "story" variant: a wider two-column card with the photograph on one side and, on the other, a short narrative paragraph (in italic serif) describing the inspiration or craftsmanship behind the piece, plus the collection name and a "Découvrir la collection" link.

FILTER/CONTEXT BAR (optional, subtle):
A slim horizontal row of soft-navy text filter pills (not heavy buttons) allowing browsing by type: Tout, Création du mois, Collection du moment, Coup de cœur, Mariage, Costume, Événement, Coulisses. Active pill uses a thin champagne underline, not a filled background.

CLOSING CTA BAND:
A full-width deep navy (#0C2650) band with centered white/ivory serif text: "Envie de porter une création Angaly ?" and a Primary button "Prendre rendez-vous" plus a Secondary outlined button "Voir toutes nos créations".

FOOTER:
Reuse the standard ANGALY footer (deep navy #041329 background, white text, link columns: Angaly, Informations, Réseaux sociaux, newsletter reminder, copyright bar with language switcher).

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Stack the editorial grid into a single column with full-bleed images, preserve large photography, allow horizontal swipe for the filter pills, keep captions concise, sticky "Prendre rendez-vous" CTA at the bottom.

AVOID:
uniform e-commerce product grids, small thumbnail-heavy layouts, excessive gold, flashy transitions, neon colors, generic blog card styling, heavy drop shadows, cluttered typography hierarchy.

The page must feel like flipping through the cover pages of a prestigious couture magazine dedicated entirely to Angaly.
```
