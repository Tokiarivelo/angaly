# Page — Collections (List)

Purpose: browse all Angaly collections (thematic/seasonal groupings of creations).

```text
Design the ANGALY collections list page — showcasing all couture collections for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar, breadcrumb: Accueil / Collections.

PAGE HEADER:
Ivory background, serif headline "Nos Collections", sans-serif subtext: "Chaque collection raconte une histoire, une saison, une inspiration."

COLLECTIONS GRID:
A generous grid (2 columns on desktop, 1 on mobile) of large collection cover cards. Each card is a tall full-bleed photograph (cover image of the collection) with a bottom gradient overlay (deep navy fading to transparent) containing: a small year/season label (e.g. "2026"), serif collection title (e.g. "Collection Éternelle"), a one-line description in ivory/white text, and a small "Découvrir la collection →" text link. Cards should feel like museum exhibition posters — minimal chrome, no borders, no rounded-SaaS corners (small radius only, 4-8px).

FEATURED COLLECTION BANNER:
Above or interspersed in the grid, one larger "Collection du moment" banner spanning full width, combining a large photo on one side and, on the other side (on ivory or navy background), the collection name in large serif, a short story paragraph, a small stat line ("12 créations"), and a Primary button "Découvrir la collection".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Single-column stacked collection cards, full-bleed images, captions remain legible with sufficient overlay contrast, tap targets large enough for touch.

AVOID:
generic e-commerce "shop by category" tile styling, excessive gold, neon colors, heavy shadows, cluttered captions.

The page should feel like an index of a prestigious fashion house's seasonal exhibitions.
```
