# Page — Accueil (Homepage)

Purpose: the main showcase page of the ANGALY couture house. Must feel like a luxury editorial magazine cover combined with a couture house website, not a generic fashion store.

```text
Design the ANGALY homepage — a premium, elegant, editorial website homepage for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT:
ANGALY is a digital couture atelier. The tone is: sophisticated, timeless, refined, luxurious, artisanal, feminine but not overly decorative, modern, editorial, exclusive, warm, trustworthy. Tagline: "Chez Angaly, votre vêtement est créé pour vous."

COLOR PALETTE (use exact hex values):
- Primary Deep Navy (dominant brand color): #061938
- Primary Dark (hover/overlay): #041329
- Navy Blue (secondary surfaces): #0C2650
- Royal Navy (borders/badges): #18375D
- Soft Navy (links/icons): #1E4574
- Ivory (main light background): #F6F2E9
- Warm Ivory (secondary light surface): #D8D3C8
- Champagne (subtle accents): #C5B190
- Antique Gold (very sparing premium accents): #936C3E
- Soft Gold (hover of gold accents): #B59A70
- Slate (secondary text): #5C697A
- Warm Gray (muted text): #8A877F
- Border: #D9D4CA
- White (limited use): #FFFFFF
Color balance target: ~60% ivory/light surfaces, 25% deep navy/blue, 10% neutrals, 5% champagne/gold accents. #061938 must dominate as the primary identity color, but do not paint the whole page navy.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Headings and editorial titles use an elegant serif similar to Cormorant Garamond (priority) or Playfair Display — large sizes, generous letter-spacing, sometimes italic for emphasis. Body text, navigation, buttons and forms use a clean modern sans-serif similar to Inter (priority) or Manrope, highly legible.

BUTTONS:
- Primary button: background #061938, text #FFFFFF, hover background #0C2650.
- Secondary button: transparent or ivory background, border and text #061938, hover fills background #061938 with white text.
- Premium button (Pattern Studio only): background #936C3E, text #FFFFFF, hover #B59A70.

TOP NAVIGATION BAR:
Sticky, ivory or deep navy background (choose deep navy #061938 with ivory/white text for a premium editorial feel), logo "ANGALY" in serif on the left. Center or left-aligned nav links in sans-serif: Accueil, La Une, Nos Créations, Prêt-à-porter, Sur Mesure, Patron Premium, À propos, Ateliers, Journal, Contact. Right-aligned icon actions: search icon, favorites/heart icon, "Mon compte" account icon, and a clearly visible primary button "Prendre rendez-vous".

SECTION 1 — HERO (full-screen, cinematic):
A full-viewport-height hero with a large, high-quality photograph of an elegant couture wedding dress or a tailored men's suit, shot editorially (soft light, elegant model or garment close-up). Apply a subtle deep navy (#061938) gradient overlay at the bottom third only, so the photography itself stays clearly visible — never fully darken the image.
Centered or bottom-left text block, in ivory/white:
- Small eyebrow label: "MAISON DE COUTURE — MADAGASCAR"
- Large serif headline: "ANGALY"
- Italic serif subheading: "L'élégance, créée pour vous."
Two buttons side by side: Primary "Prendre rendez-vous" (filled navy/white as defined above but on a photo background use white fill with navy text, or a translucent navy fill) and Secondary "Découvrir nos créations" (outlined, ivory border and text).
Add a subtle scroll-down indicator at the bottom center.

SECTION 2 — "LA UNE" (editorial highlight, immediately after hero, visually very important):
Section eyebrow: "LA UNE" with a thin champagne (#C5B190) underline rule.
Editorial headline (serif): "Les créations qui incarnent l'univers Angaly."
An asymmetrical editorial grid of 3 to 5 large photographs of different sizes (not a uniform product grid) — one large hero image plus 2–4 smaller supporting images. Each card overlays, in a small caption area (ivory background or navy background with white text depending on the card), a title such as "Collection Éclat", a category label such as "Robe de mariée — Sur mesure", and a small "Découvrir" text link with a thin arrow.
Generous whitespace between images. Use very subtle champagne accent lines or dividers only, never full champagne backgrounds.

SECTION 3 — MAISON PRESENTATION:
A two-column section on ivory background (#F6F2E9): left column a portrait-orientation photograph of the atelier or founder at work; right column short serif headline "Une maison de couture pensée pour vous", 2–3 short paragraphs about history, savoir-faire, philosophy, and specialties, followed by a Secondary button "Découvrir Angaly".

SECTION 4 — CATEGORY CARDS:
A row/grid of 5 large visual category cards with full-bleed photography and a bottom gradient caption: "Robes de mariée", "Costumes", "Robes de soirée", "Créations sur mesure", "Prêt-à-porter". Cards should have minimal rounding (small radius, not bubbly SaaS-style), subtle hover state (slight zoom on image, no heavy shadow).

SECTION 5 — SUR MESURE PROCESS BAND:
A dark navy (#0C2650) full-width band with white/ivory text showing a horizontal 7-step process with thin connecting lines and small serif step numbers: "Votre idée → Consultation → Mesures → Patron → Confection → Essayage → Livraison". Below it, a centered Primary button (white background, navy text on this dark band) reading "Créer ma tenue sur mesure".

SECTION 6 — PATTERN PREMIUM TEASER:
A premium-styled card/section using the Pattern Studio palette (#041329 background, #C5B190 accent text, #936C3E premium button) introducing: serif headline "Angaly Pattern Studio", subheading "Votre patron, créé selon vos mesures.", one short paragraph explaining it's an AI-assisted professional pattern-making atelier (not a magic dress generator), and a Premium button "Découvrir Patron Premium".

SECTION 7 — TÉMOIGNAGES:
Ivory background, serif headline "Elles nous ont fait confiance". A horizontal carousel or 3-column grid of testimonial cards: circular client photo, first name, the creation they ordered (e.g. "Robe de mariée — Collection Éternelle"), a short quote in italic serif, and a small "Avis vérifié" badge in champagne/slate where applicable.

SECTION 8 — NOS ATELIERS:
A two or three-column summary of workshop locations: photo, city/address, opening hours, and a Secondary button "Voir l'itinéraire" per card.

SECTION 9 — JOURNAL:
Serif headline "Le Journal Angaly". A row of 3–4 article preview cards: cover image, category tag (small champagne label), title in serif, publish date in slate gray.

SECTION 10 — NEWSLETTER:
A centered band on deep navy (#061938) background: serif headline "Restez informée des nouvelles collections", an email input field with rounded-minimal styling and a Primary (white) submit button "S'inscrire", plus a small consent checkbox line in warm gray.

FOOTER:
Deep navy (#041329) background, white/ivory text, organized in columns: Brand column (logo + short tagline + social icons Facebook/Instagram/TikTok), "Angaly" column (Nos créations, Collections, Sur mesure, Patron Premium, Prendre rendez-vous, Ateliers, Journal, Contact), "Informations" column (Mentions légales, CGV, Confidentialité, Cookies), and a small newsletter or contact reminder. Bottom bar with copyright and language switcher (Français / Malagasy).

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Design must also work mobile-first: hamburger menu, full-bleed imagery, stacked sections, a sticky bottom bar with a WhatsApp icon and a "Prendre rendez-vous" button, touch-friendly horizontal scroll galleries for La Une and category cards.

AVOID:
excessive gradients, excessive gold, flashy effects, neon colors, overly rounded SaaS-style cards, generic marketplace layouts, excessive shadows, overly colorful UI, stock-photo look, cluttered composition.

The final result must look like a combination of a high-end couture house, a luxury editorial magazine, and a modern digital atelier — never a generic online fashion store.
```
