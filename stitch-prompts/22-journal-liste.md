# Page — Journal (Blog List)

Purpose: the editorial blog/journal listing page covering marriage, fashion advice, trends, behind-the-scenes content.

```text
Design the ANGALY "Journal" page — the editorial blog listing page for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar with "Journal" active.

PAGE HEADER:
Ivory background, serif headline "Le Journal Angaly", sans-serif subtext: "Mariage, mode, coulisses d'atelier et conseils d'entretien."

CATEGORY FILTER BAR:
A row of slim text pills: Tout, Mariage à Madagascar, Conseils mode, Conseils costume, Tendances, Coulisses de l'atelier, Entretien des vêtements. Active pill has a thin champagne underline.

FEATURED ARTICLE:
One large featured article card at the top: big cover photo, category tag, serif title (large), short excerpt, author name + date in small slate text, "Lire l'article →" link.

ARTICLE GRID:
Below, a grid (3 columns desktop, 1 mobile) of standard article cards: cover image, small champagne category label, serif title, excerpt (1–2 lines), author avatar + name, publish date.

SIDEBAR OR BOTTOM WIDGET (optional):
A "Populaires" mini-list of 3 article titles with small thumbnails, and a compact newsletter signup card ("Recevez nos derniers articles" + email input + Primary button "S'inscrire").

PAGINATION:
Minimal pagination control or centered Secondary button "Voir plus d'articles".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Category pills become horizontally scrollable; featured article and grid become single column with full-width images.

AVOID:
generic WordPress-blog-template look, cluttered sidebars, excessive gold, heavy shadows, low-quality stock imagery.

The page should feel like a fashion magazine's editorial section — refined, readable, and visually rich.
```
