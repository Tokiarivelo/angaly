# Page — Contact

Purpose: general contact page with all channels and a simple message form.

```text
Design the ANGALY contact page for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar with "Contact" active.

PAGE HEADER:
Ivory background, serif headline "Contactez-nous", sans-serif subtext: "Une question, un projet ? Nous sommes à votre écoute."

LAYOUT — two columns:

LEFT COLUMN — CONTACT CHANNELS:
A vertical list of channel cards/rows, each with a small icon in navy or champagne: Téléphone (with click-to-call number), WhatsApp (with a direct "Discuter sur WhatsApp" button in WhatsApp's brand green kept minimal/small, or styled navy-outlined with a WhatsApp icon to stay on-brand), Email, Facebook, Instagram, and a summarized "Nos horaires" block (days/hours), plus a compact list of atelier addresses with a "Voir tous nos ateliers" link.

RIGHT COLUMN — CONTACT FORM:
A clean simple form on an ivory card with thin border: Prénom, Nom, Email, Téléphone, Sujet (dropdown: Question générale, Rendez-vous, Devis, Sur mesure, Presse, Autre), Message (textarea). Primary button, full width: "Envoyer le message".

MAP SECTION (below the two columns, full width):
An embedded map styled in muted navy/ivory tones showing all atelier locations as pins.

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Channels list stacks above the form, both full width; sticky floating WhatsApp button remains accessible at the bottom-right throughout scroll.

AVOID:
generic corporate contact-page templates, cluttered icon rows, harsh WhatsApp-green overuse clashing with the palette, excessive gold, heavy shadows.

The page should feel warm and accessible while staying visually consistent with the couture house identity.
```
