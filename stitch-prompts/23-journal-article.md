# Page — Article de Journal (Article Detail)

Purpose: single blog/journal article reading page with SEO-friendly structure and related content.

```text
Design the ANGALY journal article detail page for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope, optimized for long-form reading with comfortable line-height and measure). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar. Breadcrumb: Accueil / Journal / Conseils mode / [Article title].

ARTICLE HEADER:
Full-width cover photo (tall, editorial), small champagne category tag overlaid, large serif title overlaid or directly below on ivory background, byline row: author avatar, author name, publish date, estimated reading time, in small slate text.

ARTICLE BODY (centered narrow column, max ~680px for readability):
Sans-serif body text with generous line-height, serif pull-quotes styled in italic with a thin champagne left border for emphasis, inline full-width images breaking up long sections with small italic captions, and subheadings in serif at section breaks.

SOCIAL SHARE:
A small vertical or horizontal row of share icons (sticky on desktop scroll) — Facebook, link-copy, WhatsApp.

AUTHOR BOX:
At the end of the article, a card with author photo, name, short bio line, and a link "Voir tous ses articles".

RELATED ARTICLES:
Serif subheading "À lire aussi", a row of 3 related article cards (reuse the journal list card style).

CTA BAND:
A deep navy (#0C2650) band: serif text "Envie de concrétiser votre projet ?" with Primary button "Prendre rendez-vous".

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Cover image full-bleed, body column full-width with comfortable side padding, share icons move to a slim sticky bottom bar.

AVOID:
cramped line-length for body text, cluttered ad-like inserts, excessive gold, heavy shadows, generic blog-template typography.

The article should read beautifully — like a page from a printed fashion magazine translated to digital.
```
