# Page — 404 & Global Mobile Components

Purpose: the error page, plus the shared global mobile components (mobile navigation drawer, sticky mobile action bar, WhatsApp floating button) that should stay consistent across the whole site.

```text
Design the ANGALY 404 error page and the global mobile navigation components for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

SCREEN A — 404 PAGE:
Full-height centered layout on ivory background, minimal header (logo only). A refined, understated illustration or softly blurred fashion photograph (e.g. an empty atelier or a loose thread motif) rendered elegantly in the brand palette, not a cartoonish error graphic. Serif headline: "Cette création semble avoir disparu de l'atelier..." Sans-serif subtext: "La page que vous cherchez n'existe plus ou a été déplacée." Two buttons: Primary "Retour aux créations" and Secondary "Retour à l'accueil". Below, a small "Vous cherchiez peut-être :" row with 3 quick links (Nos Créations, Sur Mesure, Contact).

SCREEN B — MOBILE NAVIGATION DRAWER (opened from the hamburger icon):
A full-screen or slide-in drawer on deep navy (#061938) background with white/ivory text. ANGALY logo at top with a close (x) icon. A vertical list of nav links in serif or clean sans-serif, generously spaced: Accueil, La Une, Nos Créations, Prêt-à-porter, Sur Mesure, Patron Premium (with a small champagne "Premium" badge), À propos, Ateliers, Journal, Contact. Below the links, a divider, then icon-labeled quick actions: Rechercher, Mes favoris, Mon compte. At the bottom, a prominent Primary button (white background, navy text since on dark drawer) "Prendre rendez-vous" and small social icons row.

SCREEN C — STICKY MOBILE BOTTOM BAR (persistent across public pages):
A slim bottom bar (ivory or white background, thin top border #D9D4CA) with, left to right: a home icon, a search icon, a prominent centered "Prendre rendez-vous" pill button (navy fill, white text, slightly raised above the bar line), a favorites/heart icon, and an account icon. Active icon highlighted in navy, inactive icons in warm gray.

SCREEN D — FLOATING WHATSAPP BUTTON:
A circular floating action button fixed at the bottom-right of the screen (positioned to not overlap the sticky bottom bar), using a small WhatsApp icon on a navy or deep-green-adjacent circular background kept minimal to stay on-brand (prefer navy #061938 with a white WhatsApp glyph over bright WhatsApp green, to preserve the couture identity), with a subtle pulse/glow ring animation cue described (soft, slow, not flashy).

SCREEN E — MOBILE SEARCH OVERLAY:
A full-screen overlay on ivory background triggered from the search icon: a large input field at the top with a serif placeholder "Rechercher une création, un produit, un article...", below it quick category suggestion chips (Robes de mariée, Costumes, Collections, Journal), and as the user types, live grouped results (Créations, Produits, Articles) each as a small row with thumbnail and title.

AVOID:
generic cartoonish 404 illustrations, harsh bright WhatsApp green overpowering the palette, cluttered mobile drawers, excessive gold, heavy shadows.

Every component should reinforce the same premium, editorial ANGALY identity, even in these small utilitarian moments.
```
