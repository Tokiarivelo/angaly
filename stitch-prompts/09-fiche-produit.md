# Page — Fiche Produit (Ready-to-wear Product Detail)

Purpose: detail page for one ready-to-wear product, with purchase and fitting-reservation actions.

```text
Design the ANGALY product detail page ("fiche produit") for a ready-to-wear item, for ANGALY, a high-end couture fashion house based in Madagascar.

BRAND CONTEXT & PALETTE (reuse exactly):
Primary Deep Navy #061938 (dominant), Primary Dark #041329, Navy Blue #0C2650, Royal Navy #18375D, Soft Navy #1E4574, Ivory background #F6F2E9, Warm Ivory #D8D3C8, Champagne #C5B190 (subtle accents), Antique Gold #936C3E (very sparing), Slate #5C697A, Warm Gray #8A877F, Border #D9D4CA, White #FFFFFF (limited). Headings: elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display). UI/body: clean sans-serif (Inter or Manrope). Primary button navy/white. Secondary button outlined navy on ivory.

TOP NAVIGATION:
Standard ANGALY sticky nav bar including the cart icon. Breadcrumb: Accueil / Prêt-à-porter / Robes / Robe Solène.

SECTION 1 — GALLERY (left, ~55% width):
Large main product image with a thumbnail filmstrip (front, back, detail/fabric close-up, worn photo) and lightbox zoom on click. Mobile: full-bleed swipeable carousel with dot indicators.

SECTION 2 — PURCHASE PANEL (right, ~45% width, sticky on desktop):
- Small category label (e.g. "Robes de soirée")
- Serif product title: "Robe Solène"
- Reference code in small slate text: "Réf. AGL-RS-014"
- Price in navy medium-weight sans-serif, large size (e.g. "890 000 Ar")
- Availability badge (Disponible / Dernière pièce / Épuisé / Sur commande / Réservé) styled as in the catalogue
- Size selector: a row of size chips (XS, S, M, L, XL or numeric), selected state filled navy
- Color selector: circular swatches with selected state as a thin navy ring
- Material info line: "Satin duchesse, doublure soie"
- Quantity stepper (minimal, small +/- buttons)
- Primary button, full width: "Ajouter au panier"
- Secondary button, full width: "Réserver pour essayage"
- Two smaller text/icon actions: "Prendre rendez-vous" and "Ajouter aux favoris"
- A small link: "Contacter Angaly pour cette pièce"

SECTION 3 — DESCRIPTION & DETAILS:
Tabs or accordion below the two-column layout: "Description" (full descriptive paragraph), "Matière & entretien" (fabric composition, care instructions), "Livraison & retours" (delivery/return policy summary), each on ivory background with navy headings and slate body text.

SECTION 4 — SIMILAR PRODUCTS:
Serif subheading "Vous aimerez aussi", a horizontal-scroll or grid row of 4 similar product cards reusing the catalogue card style.

FOOTER:
Standard ANGALY footer.

MOBILE BEHAVIOR:
Gallery carousel on top, purchase panel stacked below (not sticky), sticky bottom action bar with Primary "Ajouter au panier" and a secondary icon button for "Réserver pour essayage".

AVOID:
flashy discount stickers, cluttered specification tables, neon "buy now" styling, excessive gold, heavy shadows.

The page should feel like a refined boutique product page — precise, elegant, and trustworthy.
```
